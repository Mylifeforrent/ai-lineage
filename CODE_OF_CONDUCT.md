# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participating in Clew to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous about the people behind them.
- Give and accept specific, evidence-based feedback; critique the change, not
  the contributor.
- Assume good faith, and ask a clarifying question before escalating.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time and may decline a contribution
  that does not fit the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments, including about the characteristics listed
  above.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, hidden backdoors or undisclosed
  telemetry.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code review, and any public space where someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct issue privately to the maintainer [@Mylifeforrent](https://github.com/Mylifeforrent)
on GitHub, or through a private report in the repository's Security tab. Do not
open a public issue about it, and do not include the details in a public
comment.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, keep the reporter's identity
confidential where possible, and choose a response proportionate to the
behavior: a private warning, a request to edit or delete content, a temporary
ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who are involved in an
incident will recuse themselves from handling it.
